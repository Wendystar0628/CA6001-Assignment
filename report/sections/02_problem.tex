\section{Problem Definition}

At planned positions $x$ and 24 depth cells $z_k$ (2--8\,m, 0.25\,m each), the regression target is
$y(x,z_k)=\ln(\qc/1\,\text{MPa})$. A \emph{low cell} has $\qc<1$\,MPa ($y<0$), and the cumulative low-cell
thickness is
\begin{equation}
H(x) = 0.25\sum_{k=1}^{24}\mathbf{1}\left[y(x,z_k)<0\right]\quad\text{(m)}.
\end{equation}
Each site is split into an initial set $S_0$, a candidate pool $C$ and a hidden audit set $A$. Starting from
$S_0$, a policy repeatedly reveals a batch from $C$ and decides whether to stop. It seeks the smallest
stopping count \Nstop{} that still meets frozen quality targets on $A$, compared with the full plan of
$\Nfull=|S_0\cup C|$ CPTs:
\begin{equation}
\min\ \Nstop\quad\text{s.t.}\quad \text{log-MAE}_A\le\tau_{\log},\quad \text{thickness-MAE}_A\le\tau_H,\quad
\Delta_{\text{full}}\le\varepsilon,
\end{equation}
where $\Delta_{\text{full}}$ is the quality loss relative to the full plan. The task thus combines \emph{spatial regression} (profiles), \emph{rare-event classification} (low cells),
\emph{active learning} (locations) and \emph{optimal stopping}.

\textbf{Constraints.}
\begin{itemize}
\item Only coordinates and already revealed curves may enter prediction, selection or stopping.
\item Near-duplicate soundings form indivisible groups.
\item Sites, not depth cells or seeds, are the replicate unit, so results are descriptive.
\item Thresholds are fixed on development sites before evaluation, and a stop that fails the audit stays a
  failure.
\end{itemize}
