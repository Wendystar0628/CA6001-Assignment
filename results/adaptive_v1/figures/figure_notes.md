# Figure evidence and export notes

- **fig01_workflow:** Investigation loop. Revealed CPTs feed prediction, batch selection and an online stop rule; the hidden audit set A is read only by a separate evaluator after the stop. All policies share S0, C and A.
- **fig02_dataset:** Data. (a) Usable CPT positions at the eight replay sites and their share of qc < 1 MPa cells. (b, c) All 2-8 m profiles at the easiest (Wellington Airport) and hardest (New Lock) evaluation sites.
- **fig03_selection_maps:** Selection maps, seed 17. Wellington Airport (a, b) at the online stop; New Lock (c, d) after the first 12-13 CPTs. Numbers give the round in which a position was revealed.
- **fig04_five_models:** Five AI methods on identical observation states at the four evaluation sites. Open marks are site means; bars and filled circles are the equal-site mean. Recall is undefined at Wellington Airport (no low-qc cells).
- **fig05_quality_vs_cpts:** Hidden-audit quality as CPTs are added (three seeds per policy). Stars mark online early stops; squares the full-plan endpoint; the red line the frozen target; the dotted line the oracle earliest passing count (uses hidden labels, diagnostic only).
- **fig06_site_outcomes:** Decision outcomes for all 48 sequential and adaptive trajectories. (a) CPTs used at the stop and the full-plan audit verdict per site; (b) CPTs saved by adaptive relative to sequential, paired by seed.
- **fig07_economics:** Owner-financial scenario, EUR 2025, adaptive policy (mean of three seeds). (a) Benefit components relative to the full plan at alpha = 1. (b) Net benefit as alpha and the daily earlier-opening benefit vary (vertical line: base case). (c) Net benefit against the decision delay per batch.

All figures use saved CSV observations and are generated by `cpt_budget.report_figures`. Site is the replicate unit: metrics are averaged within seed, then site, then equally across sites. Seed spreads are descriptive, not confidence intervals; no significance tests are used. Exports are 6.3 in wide (A4 text width): 300 dpi PNG plus editable PDF and SVG.
