# Figure evidence and export notes

- **fig01_workflow:** The investigation loop separates online decisions from hidden-label quality assessment. A frozen full candidate plan is the engineering comparator; the audit CPTs are excluded from that plan and from selection.
- **fig02_selection_map:** Matched sequential and adaptive investigations start from the same initial CPTs. The maps use the common observation count nearest 13, irrespective of quality; panel labels also give the actual final stopping count. Filled circles show revealed candidates; diamonds identify hidden audit locations. The plotted site and seed are stated in the panel.
- **fig03_five_models:** Five AI methods on a common observation sequence. Budgets and seeds are averaged within site before the equal-site mean; marks show individual site means. Recall is undefined at sites without low-resistance cells and is omitted only from that metric.
- **fig04_quality_trajectories:** Quality on the same hidden audit set as CPTs are added. Filled stop markers are the actual online triggers; crosses identify triggers that fail the frozen audit criteria. Full-plan endpoints remain visible. Audit labels do not feed the controller.
- **fig05_site_outcomes:** Every evaluation site and policy is retained. Left: planned-CPT reduction against the full plan, with completed/planned counts. A cross indicates an incorrect early stop; a square indicates no early stop. Right: the additional adaptive saving equals sequential minus adaptive completed CPTs, paired within seed. Points and ranges are site means and seed ranges; they are not confidence intervals.
- **fig06_economics:** New Lock economic scenario assessment, not observed project cash flow. The decomposition uses α = 1 and means over three seeds; whiskers and sensitivity envelopes show their range, not a confidence interval. Direct investigation savings, project-time costs, earlier-use benefits and AI/verification costs are separated. Sensitivity varies the time-transfer coefficient and daily net benefit; all signed outcomes are retained.

All figures use actual saved CSV observations. Figure 3 averages budgets and seeds within site, then sites equally; site marks and ranges show between-site variation, not confidence intervals. Figures 2 and 4 show the pre-specified New Lock case and one stated seed without an uncertainty interval. Figure 5 shows within-site seed ranges. Figure 6 shows New Lock means and ranges over the three seeds; these are descriptive ranges in a deterministic parameter scenario, not financial confidence intervals. No significance tests are used. Exports are 183 mm wide before tight bounding-box trimming; source expressions divide mm by 25.4 to obtain inches. PNG uses the requested 300 dpi report resolution; editable SVG/PDF are supplied instead of a submission TIFF.
